PRAXXT BRAND KIT
================

01-Logo
  praxxt-logo-color      Main logo. Use on white or light backgrounds.
  praxxt-logo-white      For dark backgrounds and photos.
  praxxt-logo-ink        Single colour (no gradient), e.g. one-colour printing.
  praxxt-logo-on-dark    Ready-made logo on the brand ink background.
  praxxt-logo-on-gradient Ready-made logo on the brand gradient.
  .svg files scale to any size (printers, designers). .png files are for everyday use.

02-Icon
  The P on its own, for small spaces: app icons, favicons, stickers.
  praxxt-app-icon-*.png are 1024x1024 rounded squares.

03-Social-Media
  Profile pictures (1080x1080, safe for circle crops) and banners sized for
  LinkedIn (1584x396), X/Twitter (1500x500), Facebook (1640x624) and
  YouTube (2560x1440, logo inside the safe area).

04-Website-Icons
  The browser-tab and phone home-screen icons used on praxxt.com.

05-Colours
  Colour sheet and the exact colour codes (praxxt-colours.txt).

06-Fonts
  Manrope (web font files) and its licence. For Word, PowerPoint or Canva,
  install Manrope free from https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope

07-Photos
  The student photo used on the website.

  One-page summary of how to use all of the above.
